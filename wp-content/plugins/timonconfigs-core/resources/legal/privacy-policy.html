6. How long information is kept

We keep account and access information for the period needed to operate your account and fulfill valid contractual rights. Closing an account or disconnecting Discord does not automatically erase records needed for an existing purchase, statutory obligations, fraud investigation or a legal claim.

Billing and accounting records are retained for applicable legal periods. For Polish tax records, this is generally five years from the end of the calendar year in which the relevant tax payment became due; other accounting rules, proceedings or limitation periods may require a different period. Support and dispute records are kept for the time reasonably needed to resolve the matter and handle the relevant claim period. Technical and security data are limited according to their operational or investigation purpose.

Temporary OAuth matching state is valid for five minutes and is consumed on use. Current Discord membership information is cached for up to 60 seconds before it must be checked again. These temporary checks have different retention from the underlying account or purchase history. Retention and deletion of business records may require an administrator; the existence of an account-logout button does not imply automatic deletion of all records. You may request the retention criteria applicable to a particular category.
